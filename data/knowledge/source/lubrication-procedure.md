# Lubrication Inspection Procedure

Document ID: LUB-PROC-008  
Revision: 2  
Classification: Synthetic demo data

1. Confirm the equipment is in the approved inspection state.
2. Check the lubricant type against the service record.
3. Inspect for leakage, contamination, discoloration, or over-lubrication.
4. Compare the last lubrication timestamp with the scheduled interval.
5. Record the inspection result before any controlled restart.

Do not add lubricant solely because temperature is elevated. Incorrect lubricant or
over-lubrication can increase temperature and worsen the condition.
